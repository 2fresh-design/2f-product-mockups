How it works

Four steps. From “I don't know where I stand” to a clear plan for what's next.

01
You go through a structured assessment.
No five-minute quiz. We guide you through a thought-out process that honestly maps where you stand as a designer, and you tell us where you'd like to go.
02
You get an honest read on where you really stand.
Based on the assessment: your strengths, your blind spots, and your position against the market. Concrete, no sugar-coating. Not a school grade.
03
We put together a plan for what's next.
What to learn, which projects to take on, what to change. Not a single piece of advice, but a whole path to get you where you're headed.
04
We guide you along the way.
We meet regularly, give you feedback on how you're doing, and adjust the plan as you progress. Not a one-off test, but a guide that sticks around.
